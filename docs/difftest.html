Pre-registered tolerances

Thresholds are fixed before any parity fixture is generated and may not be relaxed to make a failing test pass. Variant names and field labels are compared exactly before values; a missing field is a failure, never a default.

fixed_1 allows one unit of 1/64 pixel per scalar. coverage_8 fixes width, height, left and top exactly, admits a per-sample difference of at most 32 for 99 per cent of samples, holds ink mass within 2 per cent, allows the ink box to differ by 1 pixel, and requires non-zero winding at sizes 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 or 96.

The committed fixture toolchain adds three rules of its own: scaled bounds within 1 pixel, outline command streams exact after canonicalisation, and ink coverage within 5 per cent. Anti-aliasing is not bit-identical across rasterisers, so hinted glyphs are excluded entirely.

What is committed

Two corpora, distinguished by producer. The synthetic corpus is a deterministic byte constructor, not an oracle; the parity goldens are where another implementation supplies the answer.

  • 6 fixture categories: font, tables, cmap, metrics, outlines, coverage
  • 6 glyphs and 9 tables in synthetic.ttf
  • 5 codepoint-to-glyph-id mappings in a format 4 cmap
  • 6 outline streams and 6 coverage summaries
  • 8 SHA-256 sidecars; 64 by 64 raster grid, 8 samples per axis
  • oracle reference version 1.0.0, pure standard library
  • font digest f2e72b1c3adcf15f61e20a333d8c05eab337cae30bdd6d3932eb510faad4c0b1

The hostile-input corpus in integration/difftest/corpus/manifest.json holds 39 cases: 34 refusals and 5 accepted files, of which 4 are legal but unusual. All nine Refused variants are exercised.

  • 8 Malformed, 7 MissingTable, 6 Truncated
  • 5 OverLimit, 2 Unknown, 2 Unsupported
  • 2 Recursion, 1 Unexpected, 1 MissingGlyph

A replay cell always carries a word — match, disagrees or refused — and a mono delta. CI replays committed fixtures on wasm, wasm-gc, js and native; it never calls an oracle.

Fixtures are produced offline by reference implementations written in other languages, committed to the repository as plain data, and replayed by MoonBit tests.
